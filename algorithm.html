Confidence scoring

Confidence starts near 58–65% on the public formula alone and rises as a stylist enters real "actual packs used" feedback after appointments — each data point tightens the calibration multiplier for that stylist specifically.

What this does NOT do It does not guarantee exact usage, does not replace a stylist's professional judgment, and is not yet validated against a large real-world dataset. Confidential per-stylist calibration weights and the underlying appointment dataset are not exposed publicly — only the rule structure above is.
